Privacy policy

Docs Quick Look · effective 4 November 2026

Who we are

Docs Quick Look is a personal macOS utility made by Joe Clark, Dunckerstr. 89, 10437 Berlin, Germany (studio@joeclark.photo).

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses the Google Drive API with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. It only requests a file when you preview that file in Finder: the file's name, version and type, and an export of its contents (HTML or PDF). It also reads your Google account's email address once at sign-in, to tell your accounts apart. It never creates, changes, deletes or shares anything in your Drive.

Where your data goes

  • Nowhere except your Mac. The app talks only to Google's servers. It has no server of its own, and nobody (including the developer) receives your files, tokens or usage data.
  • Your sign-in token is stored in your Mac's login Keychain.
  • Previews are cached in ~/Library/Caches/com.joeclark.GoogleDocsQuickLook so they open quickly and work offline. Entries you haven't viewed for 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • There are no analytics, ads or tracking of any kind.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Docs Quick Look's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you previews of your own files on your own Mac. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Removing your data

  • Use Sign Out and Clear Cache in the app, or uninstall it, to delete the token and cached previews from your Mac.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: studio@joeclark.photo.
