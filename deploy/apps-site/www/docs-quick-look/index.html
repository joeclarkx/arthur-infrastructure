Docs Quick Look

A personal macOS utility by Joe Clark

Docs Quick Look is a small macOS app with a Quick Look extension. When you select a Google Docs, Sheets, Slides or Drawings file synced by Google Drive for Desktop and press Space in Finder, it shows the document's actual contents instead of the placeholder file.

How it works

  • You sign in once with your Google account and grant read-only access to Google Drive.
  • When you preview a file, the app asks the Google Drive API for that one file's export (HTML or PDF) and shows it in Quick Look.
  • Everything runs on your Mac. There is no server, no account with us, and no subscription.
